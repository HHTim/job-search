# 英文自我介紹（約 30 秒，80 個字左右）

> 2026-11-17 寫給群聯 FDE 面試（11/18）用。內容對照 ppt-studio 自我介紹簡報結尾「我能做的三件事」。

```text
Hi, I'm Tim.

I started at EVA Air, working with customers every day.
Then I learned to code and became a Java developer.

Now, at Cathay United Bank, I lead a team that brings AI
into how we build software. On our latest project,
we used about 80 percent fewer person-days.

I'm good at three things:
I ask the right questions, I build it myself,
and I get the team to really use it.

Ask, build, and bring people along.
That's what I'd love to do for you.
```

## 怎麼記

結構：從哪來 → 現在做什麼 → 一個數字 → 三個動詞。

- 最後那句「Ask, build, and bring people along」就是簡報結尾「我能做的三件事」的英文版。
- 「person-days」就是「人天」。怕對方聽不懂，可以改說「about 80 percent less work」。
- 開頭三句只是帶過經歷，忘了也沒關係。最後兩段一定要講到。
